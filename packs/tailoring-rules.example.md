# Tailoring rules (EXAMPLE)

install.sh copies this to <data dir>/config/tailoring-rules.md. Prep packs read sections 5 and 6 only; the other
sections are for you. Keep the headings exactly as written.

## 5. Role archetypes

- **implementation_deployment:** lead with go-lives, migrations and on-time delivery.
- **embedded_ai_strategist:** lead with AI work done inside a business team.
- **technical_enablement:** lead with training and documentation.
- **pilot_led_deployment:** lead with pilots that turned into rollouts.

## 6. Allowed vocabulary translations

- "data pull" may be written as "data pipeline" only where the line describes an automated, recurring job.
- "stakeholders" may be written as "cross-functional partners".
